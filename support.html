Reporting an ad

If an ad was inappropriate, misleading or wrong for the game's age rating, there is a Report an ad button inside the app under Privacy & ads on the main menu. It opens a prefilled email. Tell me roughly when you saw it and what it showed, and include a screenshot if you have one, and I will report it to Google.

Common questions

My progress disappeared

Wins and unlocked decks are stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is synced. Deleting the app, or wiping its data, erases them. Reinstalling starts fresh.

A challenge code will not load

Codes are long, and some chat apps break them across lines or clip the end. Ask your friend to send it again on its own, with nothing else in the message.

Do I have to allow tracking?

No. Declining means you see non-personalised ads instead. The game is identical either way — nothing is gated behind it.

The opponent is too fast

Change it on the main menu under Opponent. Easy is genuinely beatable while you are still learning the rules. There is also a guided tutorial under Learn to play.
