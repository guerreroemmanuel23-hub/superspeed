Super Speed — Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Super Speed is a single-player card game published by Emmanuel Guerrero. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, who else receives it, and what you can do about it. It is short because the app does very little.

What the app itself collects

Nothing that leaves your device. Super Speed has no account, no sign-in, and no server of its own. The app stores the following locally, on your device only:

  • Your number of wins, current streak and best streak
  • Which card decks you have unlocked and which one is selected
  • Your sound setting, chosen difficulty and chosen deck
  • The display name you type for ghost-race challenges, and your win/loss record against names you have raced

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It is not backed up to us, because there is no “us” to back it up to. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Ghost-race challenge codes contain a shuffle seed, a deck and difficulty, a finish time, a pace record and whatever display name you typed. They are created on your device and shared only by you, to whoever you choose to send them to. Nothing about a challenge passes through any server we operate.

Advertising

The free version of Super Speed shows ads between games, supplied by Google AdMob. To select and measure those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS)
  • Your approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Device and app information, and interactions with the ads themselves
  • Diagnostic information such as crash and performance data

Google acts as an independent controller of that data, and its handling of it is governed by Google’s own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. A description of how Google uses data from apps that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We require, through Google’s publisher terms, that any partner receiving data through the app provides protection equivalent to what is described here.

Your choices

  • Tracking. The first time the app runs, iOS asks whether Super Speed may track you across apps and websites. If you say no, Google does not receive your advertising identifier and you see non-personalised ads. The game is identical either way. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Reset your advertising identifier. Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Apple Advertising let you limit and reset it.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). Where required, a consent prompt appears before any ad loads, and your choice is remembered. You can withdraw or change it by reinstalling the app, or by contacting us at the address below and asking us to walk you through it.

Children

Super Speed is not directed at children and is not published in the App Store’s Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact us and we will address it.

Retention and deletion

Because the app keeps everything on your device and sends nothing to us, we hold no data about you and therefore have nothing to retain or delete. To erase what the app has stored locally, delete the app from your device. Data held by Google as a result of ad serving is retained under Google’s policies, and Google provides its own controls for it at myadcenter.google.com.

Reporting an ad

If an ad in Super Speed was inappropriate, misleading or unsuitable for the game’s age rating, tell us and we will report it to Google. There is a Report an ad button inside the app under Privacy & ads, or you can email us directly.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
